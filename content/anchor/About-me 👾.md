# Thinkpad T60
---

![t60](t60.png)

When i got this machine it was far from functional , windows vista was preinstalled , chrome refused to run on the 2 gigs of ram this thing had.
This was also when 32 bit machines were being phased out.

This machine really allowed me to tinker and keep pushing it to its limits.
i kept finding ways to make this machine as usable as possible and this lead me to a VARIETY of tinkering and exploring:
- This machine is the reason i found out about FOSS : Linux , VLC , Krita , KDE , GNOME , VIM.
- More importantly , it really made me appreciate the FOSS community. when your machine runs on free and open source software you **truly own** the machine

Even today , the reason i love computers is because of the FOSS community. 
Free and open source software is the essence that breathes life and gives philosophical meaning to computers.
